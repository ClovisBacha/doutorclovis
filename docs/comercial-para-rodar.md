# PARA RODAR — todos os prompts, em ordem

Folha de execução. Só prompts, sem explicação. O **porquê** de cada escolha está
em [`roteiro-comercial.md`](./roteiro-comercial.md) — este arquivo é para copiar
e colar.

**Ordem importa.** Rode de cima para baixo. Cada bloco diz o que anexar.

## ⚠️ Se der "Rejected due to copyright restrictions"

Todos os prompts abaixo já estão limpos. Se ainda assim algum for barrado,
remova nesta ordem — a primeira remoção quase sempre resolve:

1. A palavra **`Brazilian`** (nacionalidade + rosto às vezes dispara)
2. A palavra **`photographic realism`** → troque por `natural photography`
3. A linha **`Fictional character designed for this production.`** inteira
4. **`pore-level texture`** → troque por `natural skin texture`

**Nunca** escreva nos prompts: `public figure`, `real person`, `the exact
person/woman/man from the reference`, `model`, `in the style of`, `film still`,
nome de marca, nome de câmera ou de lente.

## PLANO 1 · A NOTÍCIA

**Anexar:** a mãe escolhida + o cenário banheiro

```
Photographic realism, 4K, anamorphic 2.39:1, intimate drama grade, creamy
highlight roll-off, fine film grain. Fictional character designed for this
production.

LIGHTING: single cool overcast morning key through a frosted window at
camera-LEFT, pale and slightly blue, weak wraparound fill from white tile. No
sun, no warmth, no practical light anywhere.

COLOR: 60:30:10 — cool pale grey-blue (tile, porcelain, daylight) 60% /
light-brown skin and dark hair 30% / off-white cotton 10%. NOTHING warm in
frame. This is the coldest shot in the film.

CAMERA: anamorphic cine optics, 50mm, shallow depth, ONE extremely slow push-in
that never stops and never cuts. 180 degree shutter, real motion blur.

OPTICS: opens MCU 29 degrees already close on her face and hands, easing to CU
24 degrees.

SKIN: pore-level texture — fine peach-fuzz along the jaw, real catch-lights in
the eyes, tear film gathering on the lower lid, a faint natural flush rising on
the cheeks, no smoothing.

ACTING: the whole shot happens on her face, and it holds only THREE states — not
four. We arrive with the trembling smile ALREADY breaking across her face; do
NOT show the moment she first reads the test. The smile grows and her eyes fill.
Then it falters, and something frightened arrives in her eyes and STAYS there —
this beat gets real time, it is not a flicker. Finally she breathes it away, and
the smile that returns is smaller and much more real than the first one.

PHYSICS: real breath moving her chest; a curl of hair falling forward; her hands
trembling very slightly; a small white plastic stick held in both hands.

FIRST FRAME: the woman sitting on the edge of a bathtub in a cold bathroom, head
bowed, holding a small white plastic stick in both hands, already looking down
at it, cool daylight from the LEFT.

ACTION — ONE CONTINUOUS SHOT, 8 seconds, no cuts:
0:00-0:02  We arrive mid-break. The trembling disbelieving smile is ALREADY on
           her face and still growing. Her eyes fill.
0:02-0:04  The smile falters. Fear arrives in her eyes.
0:04-0:05  The fear STAYS. She does not fight it yet. She just holds it.
0:05-0:07  She breathes it away. The smile returns, smaller, steadier, real.
0:07-0:08  She lifts one hand off it and lays it flat and open against her own
           stomach, and holds it there, completely still.

LAST FRAME: her open hand resting flat on her stomach, held still, her face soft
and half out of focus above it. Clean and held for the final 0.5s.

AUDIO (NO MUSIC): a quiet apartment in the morning — faint rain outside, the hum
of a building, her one shaky inhale at 0:01. Nothing else.

POSITIVE LOCKS: same character design throughout, same bathroom. Window stays on
the LEFT. NO warm light of any kind anywhere. The camera NEVER cuts, NEVER
stops, NEVER pulls back. The final 0.5s is still and clean.

NEGATIVE: no music, no dialogue, no text, no letters, no numbers, no warm light,
no practical lights, no camera shake, no stutter, no choppy or dropped frames,
no strobing.
```
